Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Starwake Protocol handles information when you use the game and its website.

Information stored by the game

Starwake Protocol stores game saves, settings, upgrade history, recovery data, and similar gameplay information locally in your browser. This information is used to operate the game and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer by the game itself. Clearing browser storage may erase this data.

Advertising and cookies

This site may use Google AdSense and other advertising providers. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, limit, and measure advertisements. Google's advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You may also learn about opting out of some third-party personalized advertising through AboutAds.

Consent choices

Where required, this site uses Google's certified Consent Management Platform through AdSense Privacy & messaging. The message lets eligible visitors consent, decline consent, or manage individual options before advertising data is used as configured. Google and participating advertising providers may store the resulting consent signals so the selection can be honored and later changed.

Information advertising providers may process

Depending on your location, consent choices, browser, and the ads displayed, advertising providers may process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location;
  • browser, device, operating-system, and language information;
  • cookie or advertising identifiers;
  • pages viewed, ad impressions, interactions, and referral information;
  • information used to detect fraud, abuse, and invalid traffic.

Advertising providers process data under their own privacy policies. For more information, review how Google uses information from partner sites and Google's advertising technologies.

Analytics and operational logs

The current game does not intentionally send gameplay save data to a private developer server. Hosting, security, analytics, or advertising services may nevertheless create standard technical logs or measurement records when you access the site.

Children's privacy

Starwake Protocol is not directed specifically to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the game. Advertising settings and applicable consent requirements should be configured appropriately before serving ads to younger audiences.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • You can clear Starwake Protocol save data through the game or browser storage controls.
  • You can select Privacy & Cookie Settings on this page to reopen Google's consent choices after the integration is activated.
  • You can opt out of personalized Google advertising using Google Ads Settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the game, hosting, advertising, analytics, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be submitted through the project's public issue tracker at the Starwake Protocol GitHub repository. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Consent control: The Privacy & Cookie Settings button is wired to Google's official consent-revocation interface. It becomes active when the approved AdSense site tag is installed and the European regulations message is published.
